# Sales Executive Summary Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance across regions, product categories, and salespeople — paired with a structured business insights page that translates the data into findings and actionable recommendations.

## Objective
This project explores company-wide sales data to identify top-performing categories and regions, uncover the drivers behind performance gaps, and provide recommended business actions based on the findings — not just visualize numbers, but explain what they mean.

## Data Source
Sales dataset covering product categories, regions, salespeople, and monthly order/revenue records for 2025.

## Key Insights & Analysis

**What's happening:**
- Revenue of 88M with 23M profit (25.63% margin)
- Laptops are the dominant category at 48M — far ahead of every other category
- Top regions: Balochistan, Sindh, and Punjab; KPK is the weakest performer

**Why it's happening:**
- Laptops carry a higher price per unit, driving outsized revenue impact
- Top regions likely have stronger distribution/sales presence
- A later-month revenue spike suggests a seasonal buying pattern

**Key trends:**
- Laptops dominate both revenue and volume
- Just 3 provinces drive the majority of total revenue
- Salesperson performance varies significantly despite similar order counts

**Areas that need attention:**
- Over-reliance on a single category (laptops) is a risk
- KPK is underperforming and needs investigation
- Noticeable performance gap between top and bottom salespeople

**Recommended business actions:**
- Push bundles/promotions on weaker categories (Gaming, Office Equipment, Smart Devices)
- Investigate and invest in growing the KPK market
- Add a returns breakdown by category/region for root-cause tracking
- Coach low-performing salespeople using top performers' methods
- Plan inventory/staffing ahead of seasonal peak months
- Set a target to bring return rate below 4%

## Tools & Skills Used
- Power BI (data modeling, dashboard design)
- DAX measures (profit margin %, return rate %, revenue aggregations)
- Power Query (data transformation)
- Data visualization (bar charts, donut charts, KPI cards, trend lines)
- Business analysis (root-cause thinking, actionable recommendations)
